# Acme Corporation - Employee Handbook

## Remote Work Policy

Acme Corporation allows all full-time employees to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Remote work days must be approved by the employee's direct manager at least 48 hours in advance. Employees working remotely are expected to be available on Slack between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM in their local time zone.

Employees who wish to work fully remote must submit a formal request to the Human Resources department. Fully remote arrangements are reviewed on a quarterly basis and may be revoked if performance metrics are not met.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per year, accrued at a rate of 1.67 days per month. PTO requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks before the requested dates. Managers have 3 business days to approve or deny PTO requests.

Unused PTO can be carried over to the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any PTO beyond this limit will be forfeited on January 1st. Employees who leave the company will be paid out for any unused, accrued PTO at their current daily rate.

## Parental Leave

Acme Corporation provides 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for all new parents, regardless of gender. This applies to birth, adoption, and foster care placements. Parental leave must begin within 12 months of the qualifying event. Employees may choose to take parental leave in two separate blocks, with manager approval.

## Health Insurance

Acme Corporation offers three health insurance plans: Bronze, Silver, and Gold. The company covers 80% of the premium for the Silver plan, which is the default enrollment option. Employees may upgrade to the Gold plan by paying the difference, approximately $150 per month. Dental and vision coverage are included at no additional cost in all plans.

Open enrollment occurs annually during the month of November. Life changes such as marriage, birth of a child, or relocation allow for mid-year enrollment changes within 30 days of the qualifying event.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted semi-annually in June and December. Each review consists of a self-assessment, peer feedback from at least two colleagues, and a manager evaluation. Ratings are on a scale of 1 to 5, where 3 indicates "Meets Expectations" and 5 indicates "Exceptional Performance."

Employees who receive a rating of 4 or above are eligible for a merit-based salary increase of 3% to 8%. Employees who receive a rating below 2 for two consecutive review periods will be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 90 days.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of the transaction. Receipts are required for all expenses over $25. The following categories have pre-approved limits:
- Meals during business travel: up to $75 per day
- Hotel accommodations: up to $200 per night
- Ground transportation: up to $50 per day
- Conference registration: up to $2,000 per event per year

Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days and deposited directly into the employee's paycheck.
