# Acme Corporation - IT Security Policy

## Password Requirements

All employees must use passwords that are at least 14 characters long and include a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The system will not allow reuse of the last 12 passwords. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company systems, including email, VPN, and cloud applications.

## Data Classification

Acme Corporation classifies data into four levels:
1. Public: Information intended for public release, such as marketing materials.
2. Internal: General business information not intended for public disclosure, such as internal memos.
3. Confidential: Sensitive business data including financial reports, strategic plans, and employee records.
4. Restricted: Highly sensitive data such as trade secrets, customer payment information, and access credentials.

Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Sharing of Restricted data outside the organization requires written approval from the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).

## Acceptable Use Policy

Company devices and networks are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted as long as it does not interfere with work responsibilities or violate any company policies. Employees must never:
- Install unauthorized software on company devices
- Connect personal USB storage devices to company computers
- Access or store inappropriate or illegal content on company systems
- Share login credentials with anyone, including IT staff

## Incident Reporting

All security incidents must be reported to the IT Security team within 1 hour of discovery by emailing security@acmecorp.com or calling the Security Hotline at extension 4455. Examples of security incidents include suspected phishing emails, unauthorized access attempts, lost or stolen devices, and suspected malware infections.

The IT Security team will classify the incident and respond within 4 hours for Critical incidents and 24 hours for Medium severity incidents. Failure to report a known security incident is a violation of company policy and may result in disciplinary action.

## Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Employees may use personal devices for work purposes only after enrolling them in the Mobile Device Management (MDM) system. Personal devices must have a screen lock enabled, full-disk encryption, and up-to-date operating systems. The company reserves the right to remotely wipe corporate data from personal devices if the device is lost, stolen, or if the employee leaves the company.
